# 🌿 AI Health Assistant — Health Awareness Chatbot

A modern AI-powered Health Awareness Chatbot built with **HTML, CSS, JavaScript** and **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash API**.

## ✨ Features

- 🤖 Powered by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash AI
- 🥗 Nutrition & healthy eating guidance
- 🏃 Exercise & fitness routines
- 😴 Sleep & recovery tips
- 🧠 Mental health & stress management
- 🛡️ Disease prevention & wellness habits
- 💧 Hydration & lifestyle advice
- ⚡ Quick-topic chips for instant answers
- 📱 Fully responsive (mobile + desktop)
- ⚠️ Built-in safety guardrails (no diagnosis/treatment claims)

## 🚀 Run Locally

### Option 1 — One Click
Double-click `START_SERVER.bat`

### Option 2 — Manual
```bash
node server.js
```
Then open → **http://localhost:3000**

## 🛡️ Safety Disclaimer

This chatbot provides **general health education only**.  
It does **NOT** diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional medical advice.  
For emergencies, always call your local emergency services.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JS |
| AI Engine | Google Gemini 2.0 Flash |
| Server | Node.js (built-in `http` module) |
